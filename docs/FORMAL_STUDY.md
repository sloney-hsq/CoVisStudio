# Prospective two-condition study: materials and assignment contract

Protocol `covis-formal-study-0.1`; materials `covis-formal-materials-0.1`.
These are new reviewable `study_01_a`–`study_04_b` materials. They do not relabel or
replace the authored `biz_*`, `rev_*` or `exp_*` diagnostics and their fixed answers.
The [unified protocol](UNIFIED_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md) governs the research question.
**Human material review, voice feasibility/listening, annotation rubrics, meaningful-effect
and precision thresholds, consent and analysis-plan approval remain pending.** A
reproducible manifest hash records the candidate content; it is not an approval.

The comparison concerns whether visible, correctable separation of evidence origin,
current edits, and retained/released conditions helps people understand and correct
revisions. It does not compare binding algorithms or input modalities. An automatic
first action before the participant uses the support cannot show a card benefit.

## Eight materials with participant choices

All materials start with three real Olist charts: orders, low-review rate and mean
delivery time. The existing authored histories are clearly labelled starting evidence.
They were not generated by the participant or their model session. The public brief
contains a business goal, machine-readable hard constraints, open choices and the same
six-field response form: recommendation, evidence, object origin, target charts,
calculation scope, and retained/released conditions. No expected category list, `expected_sources`, prescribed
reference phrase, or required interruption enters a formal brief.

| Family | Business purpose | Verifiable final business constraints | Participant decisions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Choose a regional service investigation | Views 2/3 retain service measures and current region, 2017 and delivered status; same categories; view 1 unchanged | Historical, current or other supported members; fixed selection or ranking; retaining/narrowing/replacing it |
| 02 | Choose local operating priorities | All three views retain current region, 2017, delivered status and their respective measures; same categories | Keep or release the inherited selection; choose members or ranking measure/count |
| 03 | Investigate first-/third-quarter service differences | Views 2/3 retain third-quarter regional delivered scope and service measures; same categories; view 1 unchanged | Comparison cohort and selection rule; revisions after inspecting the evidence; whether a change claim is supported |
| 04 | Review financial and service priorities | View 1 uses revenue excluding freight; views 2/3 retain service measures; same chosen categories, current region, 2017 and delivered status | Keep, narrow or replace the starting cohort and choose its selection rule; whether revenue changes the recommendation |

Versions A/B exchange RJ and MG. Families 01/02 use RJ history → MG current in A,
and MG history → RJ current in B. Families 03/04 use RJ in A and MG in B; family 03
compares January–March with July–September 2017. A period's end is exclusive in the
source contract. Starting charts, history depth, chart count and hard-constraint kinds
match within each pair. Human difficulty equivalence has not been established: actual
rank changes, conflicts between metrics and review denominators must be inspected in
the human material review. Structural matching is not a substitute for that review.

Hard constraints use three simple public forms:

```json
{"kind":"source_fields","view_number":2,"required":{"region":"MG","metric":"low_review_rate"}}
{"kind":"unchanged_view","view_number":1}
{"kind":"same_members","view_numbers":[2,3]}
```

The full source constraints also contain dates, delivered status and category dimension.
They deliberately omit `categories`, `top_n` and `rank_by`. Equal observed members alone
do not establish that the participant's intended fixed/ranking rule was followed.
That rule, changed targets, retained conditions and evolving intent come from the
participant's words, explicit choices and contemporaneous evidence, independently
annotated before outcomes are inspected. Numerical checks then use that intention and
independent SQL. Hard-constraint completion, intention correctness, understanding and
business interpretation remain separate. The final chart cannot supply missing intent.

## Fixed treatment and voice window

Only `direct` and `bound_revision` are assigned, both in `live_voice` using the same
`qwen3.8-omni-flash-realtime` model. History, images, saved-set creation/listing/application,
candidate resolution, compiler, cancellation and commit permissions are shared. Native
history is retained with exact outbound archives; it is not described as frozen to
what the participant heard. B2 exposes correctable origin/edit/retained-released content;
B0 retains the same execution and saved-set capabilities. Ambiguous choices follow the
same clarification rule, and explicit edits need no additional confirmation click.

Each trial has a 480-second Begin-to-terminal limit. Within that interval the participant
chooses when to start one explicit live-voice connection after initial inspection and
before finishing. That connection lasts at most 60 seconds. Its initial discussion,
explanation and any participant-chosen revision all share this window. A participant
is not required to interrupt, use a deictic phrase or produce an overlap episode.
Before/after voice, the remaining task time supports offline inspection, manual chart
operations and the written response. It does not grant another voice connection or
text-model window. Closing early does not authorize a replacement connection. A failed
window remains part of the assigned attempt. A separately justified trial repeat is
recorded as a repeat, not a way to extend or replace the primary outcome.

The runtime must preserve the assigned task, condition, transport and budget for the
trial. Opening the plan or preparing charts opens no provider. Real API calls require
their separate authorization; this implementation and its tests make none. The actual
60-second presentation/revision fit and acoustic/listening quality still need a bounded
human feasibility review before formal collection.

## Assignment and primary attempts

The existing 30-slot plan is retained: four different families per person, two trials
per condition, two A and two B variants. The 16-slot Williams family-order × condition-block
order × variant-pattern cycle is unchanged. Thirty slots yield 60 assignments per
condition and joint cells of one or two, not perfect balance or a power guarantee.
Each formal trial has a distinct `study-sNNN-tN` ID, material hash and assignment hash.
The 30-slot plan is a schedule, not thirty observed or registered people.

An actual pseudonymous roster of `{participant_id, participant_slot}` supplies the
analysis denominator. `formal_primary_attempts(records, registrations)` consumes raw
session exports, including `report=null`, and creates one row for every registered
assignment. It selects the earliest actual `trial_started` event in a formal record
with `evidence_kind=participant`. Preparation time, local authored runs, technical
scores and later success do not choose the primary. Every later Begin is a repeat;
its reason is recorded separately. Prepared-only attempts remain prepared, while their
assignment is missing a primary. No-archive participants still contribute four missing
rows. Unfinished, timed-out, abandoned and unscored first attempts remain primary.

The function returns `assigned_rows`, `attempt_rows`, `primary_attempts`, `missing_trials`,
all original records and separate non-primary-protocol/evidence records. It does not
edit the archives or fill human outcomes. `formal_assignment_csv` exports every assigned
row, including missing trials; it is not an actual-attempt-only CSV. The old
`aggregate_reports` and its latest-attempt summaries retain their historical descriptive
meaning and must not substitute for the formal primary table.

The application integration uses the explicit formal plan selector
`/api/experiment/plan?protocol=formal`, stores the complete returned assignment in
`session.assignment`, and archives the public task. Formal task setup is dispatched
to `formal_setup_operations`; formal reporting uses hard constraints and the independent
intent workflow, never the old authored `expected_sources`. The default older plan
remains distinguishable. The formal manifest should be saved with the collection
configuration and actual roster before collection; hashes bind the reviewed content.

## Human freeze and analysis decisions still required

Before collection, reviewers must inspect all eight real-data materials, execute both
shared flows, hear the intended 60-second presentation/revision window, and record any
material changes followed by a new manifest hash. They must approve the intent and
understanding rubrics, consent scope, unknown handling, rerun reasons and analysis plan.
No code function produces an approval or an actual participant result.

The planned independent unit is the participant. Account for family, version and
order, retaining all assigned tasks and reporting missing/unknown reasons. Understanding
and final revision correctness are the two primary outcomes; any confirmatory tests
need joint adjustment (for example Holm), effect sizes and participant-clustered
uncertainty. Report known success and unknown counts plus lower/upper success bounds.
Repair and time are secondary; failed or time-limited attempts must not disappear from
timing analysis. Thirty is the existing recruitment target: the meaningful effect,
variance/precision rationale and interval-width thresholds require a human decision
before outcome inspection. None is silently supplied by software.

Local verification is recorded under `traces/formal-study-materials/`. Tests cover the
new material/assignment contracts, real Olist setup, first actual Begin selection,
unscored and failed primary attempts, repeats, and roster-derived missing rows. These
checks do not establish human difficulty, listening, natural revision frequency or a
treatment effect.


## Terminal intent and repair analysis clarification

The fixed primary outcomes are final-intent consistency and interface-assisted
interpretation (final revision correctness and understanding). High pilot accuracy
must not trigger replacement of either primary after inspecting outcomes.

The human terminal lock includes ALL still-active per-view requirements, including
earlier unfulfilled requests. A later scoped goal change supersedes only the affected
requirements; superseded goals are not required at task end, and earlier legal commits
remain legitimate. `intent.view_overrides` maps a target view ID to optional
`source_patch`, `members`, `retained_fields`, and `released_fields` overrides using the
existing coding fields. Thus view 2 may release fixed members for a revenue ranking
while view 3 retains a fixed cohort. Annotations and SQL oracles are private and never
enter model input or public tool schemas. Missing or unresolved intent remains unknown.

Episode order uses original onset event indices, not annotation creation time. A later
annotated analytical episode, including unknown intent, blocks an older terminal
selection. Adding a newer episode makes an existing older selection unknown until
reviewed. Coders must inspect coverage of all participant analytical changes before
locking the terminal intent; this ordering check does not infer unannotated goals,
classify ASR text, or certify human coverage. Existing earlier episode scores remain
historical local assessments, not simultaneous terminal goals.

Manual coding distinguishes repair, deliberate goal change and exploration. Report
the repair funnel with explicit denominators: all assigned tasks; human-confirmed
initial errors; correction attempts; verified successful corrections. Retain unknowns
at each stage. These post-treatment subsets are descriptive, not causal comparisons
among error cases, card users or successful repairs. All assignments remain primary.
A card cannot influence an action before actual exposure and opportunity to use it;
this is not blanket first-commit parity after earlier support in a multi-step task.
